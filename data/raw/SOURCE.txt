NYC Open Data | 311 Service Requests from 2020 to Present

API: https://data.cityofnewyork.us/resource/erm2-nwe9.csv
SELECT unique_key,created_date,complaint_type,descriptor,incident_zip,borough
WHERE created_date >= '2024-04-01T00:00:00' AND created_date < '2024-10-01T00:00:00' AND complaint_type like 'Noise%' AND borough='MANHATTAN'
ORDER BY unique_key | LIMIT 200000
Rows: 102859, downloaded 2026-11-11 (Asia/Seoul).
One row per NYC 311 service request. created_date is the source wall-clock time, interpreted as New York local time.
complaint_type is labeled Problem in the NYC Open Data portal. ZIP values are identifiers.
